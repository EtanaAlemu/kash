Offline-first expense tracking. Log spending and income, set a monthly limit, and keep your data encrypted on your phone — not in our cloud.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Built for privacy

Transactions, categories, and budgets stay on your device in an encrypted local database. Optional PIN or biometric lock, backups you control, and a daily reminder you can turn off.

What leaves the phone

  • Crash diagnostics only (Firebase Crashlytics), to help fix bugs
  • No account-based sync of your expense history to Kash servers
